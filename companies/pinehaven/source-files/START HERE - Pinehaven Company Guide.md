# Finance & Operations Workspace Guide

Pinehaven Motion Systems, Inc. is a privately held Delaware manufacturer of
industrial motion-control equipment. This workspace reflects the June 30,
2026 pre-close position; May 2026 is the latest closed month.

## Operating footprint

- Site 100 — Grand Rapids, Michigan: headquarters, final assembly, engineered systems
- Site 200 — Dayton, Ohio: machining, gears, and shafts
- Site 300 — Greenville, South Carolina: controls, electronics, and cabinet integration
- Site 400 — Fort Worth, Texas: aftermarket distribution and service inventory

## Product families

- DRV servo gear drives
- ACT electric linear actuators
- CTL motion-control cabinets
- SYS engineered motion skids
- AFT aftermarket kits and spares

## Source authority

The ERP is authoritative for transactions, balances, and master data. Approved
policies govern accounting method; signed contracts govern binding legal terms;
approved forecast versions govern prospective planning. Files in this workspace
include workpapers, policies, presentations, exports, and communications. When
dates or versions differ, reconcile the document to the applicable ERP report
before relying on it.
